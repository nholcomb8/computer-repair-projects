# HP Pavilion 15 – Battery Replacement & System Refresh

## Overview

This project involved diagnosing and repairing an HP Pavilion 15 laptop with a swollen battery. The battery was replaced, and the laptop was then cleaned up and prepared for continued use.

## Issue

The laptop had a swollen battery that needed to be replaced due to the potential for further hardware damage and safety concerns. The trackpad was not functioning due to the battery bulging.

## Repair

- Removed the swollen battery from the laptop.
- Installed a replacement battery.
- Inspected the laptop after the battery replacement to ensure everything was properly seated and reassembled.

## System Preparation

After completing the hardware repair, I prepared the laptop for use again:

- Cleared the previous user's data from the system.
- Performed a factory reset of Windows.
- Installed available Windows updates.
- Installed required system and hardware updates.
- Verified that the laptop was functioning properly after the reset and updates.

## Result

The swollen battery was successfully replaced, and the laptop was returned to a clean, updated, and usable state.
