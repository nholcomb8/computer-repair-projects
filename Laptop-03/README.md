# Lenovo G580 – Laptop Teardown & Parts Recovery

## Overview

This project involved disassembling a Lenovo G580 laptop with a broken display to recover usable hardware components for future repair projects.

## Condition

The laptop had a broken screen and was no longer practical to repair as a complete system. I decided to disassemble the laptop and retain usable components for potential future use.

## Teardown

I carefully disassembled the laptop and removed the following components:

- Hard drive
- RAM
- Optical disc drive
- Processor

The components were removed and retained as potential replacement parts for future computer repair projects.

## Skills Demonstrated

- Laptop disassembly
- Hardware identification
- Component removal
- Hardware inspection
- Parts recovery and organization
